Dispute Desk

Turn failed African fintech transactions into a ready complaint in 60 seconds, then track your bank's response window.

About Dispute Desk

Dispute Desk is an AI-powered financial complaint advocate based in Lagos, Nigeria, designed for consumers across Nigeria and South Africa dealing with failed bank transfers, uncredited debits, POS dispensing errors, and delayed merchant reversals.

The platform bridges the communication gap between frustrated consumers and institutional dispute desks by extracting critical transaction forensic data (Session IDs, RRNs, references) from screenshots or receipts and formatting legally referenced complaint letters cited against the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Consumer Protection Framework and the South African National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFOSA).

How It Works

  1. 1. Forensic Receipt Extraction: Upload any bank receipt, debit alert, or POS slip. Claude Vision parses session IDs, dates, amounts, and settlement references with account numbers automatically masked to the last 4 digits for privacy.
  2. 2. Verified Provider Routing: Identify the correct dispute desk and escalation inbox across 16 verified banks and fintechs in Nigeria and South Africa.
  3. 3. Statutory Complaint Generation: Generate a formal, citation-backed complaint letter tailored in courteous, firm, or regulatory ultimatum tone with 1-click email dispatch or PDF export.
  4. 4. Response Window Tracking: Monitor provider resolution deadlines with built-in SLA timers and 1-tap regulatory escalation to the CBN Consumer Protection Department or the Banking Ombud if unresolved.
